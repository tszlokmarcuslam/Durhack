Nook Meet Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026. Nook Meet is a student project made at DurHack 2026.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address, and your name and profile picture if you sign in with Google. Stored in our database (Supabase).
  • Profile: the username, display name, avatar, tags, city, university and other details you choose to add. Each detail is private unless you switch on sharing in Settings.
  • Friends and parties: who you add as a friend and which parties you join.
  • Companion chats: messages you send to the AI companion.

Camera and microphone

Body tracking runs in your browser. Your camera picture is never uploaded or stored. During a call, only the positions of your avatar's body are sent to the other person. Call voice goes directly between the two browsers and is not recorded.

Services we use

  • Supabase: accounts, profiles, friends and live connections between players.
  • Google Gemini: answers your companion messages. Your message and recent chat are sent to Google to get a reply.
  • ElevenLabs: turns companion replies into speech when voice is on.
  • Render: hosts this website.

We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Your choices

You can edit your profile and sharing settings at any time. To delete your account and data, email us and we will remove it.
