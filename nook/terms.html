Using Nook Meet

  • You must be at least 18 years old.
  • Be kind. No harassment, hate speech, sexual content, threats or spam in chats, calls or your profile.
  • Do not try to break, overload or misuse the service, or access other people's accounts.

We may remove content or accounts that break these rules.

Calls with strangers

Random calls connect you with people you do not know. Do not share personal information you would not want a stranger to have, and leave any call that makes you uncomfortable.

AI companion

The companion's answers come from an AI and can be wrong. Do not rely on them for medical, legal or financial decisions.
